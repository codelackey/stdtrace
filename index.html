STD Switching & Trace
STD Switching & Backfeed Simulator Guide

1. Click Action Modes:

  • Toggle Open/Close: Click any Substation Breaker, Recloser/Fuse, Switch, or Transformer to open or close it.
  • Trace Upstream / Downstream: Click any element to highlight its live path to the active source or downstream loads.

2. Try a Backfeed Restoration Scenario:

  1. Click REC-01 (blue diamond) to open it and simulate an outage on Feeder 1.
  2. Click SW-TIE (hollow square) to close the tie switch and restore Feeder 1 via an amber backfeed from Substation 2.
  3. Close REC-01 while SW-TIE is still closed to trigger a magenta parallel loop warning.
